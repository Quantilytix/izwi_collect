IZWI

Recording your voice

We are building a synthetic version of your voice so Smart-Q can speak its replies in Shona, in a voice its users already recognise. Nothing about this recording session is automatic: you agree to it first, you review every clip before it leaves your phone, and a person checks it again before it is ever used to train anything.

BEFORE YOU RECORD
  • Read the consent screen in full and ask questions before you agree to anything.
  • Pick a quiet room. Use the same seat and the same distance from the phone every session.
  • Speak the way you actually talk to a customer, not slower, not more formal.

Each recording session

  1. Accept the consent screen once. Enter your name or ID, the app remembers it for the whole session.
  2. One sentence at a time. Read the prompt on screen aloud after you tap Record.
  3. A sentence read oddly or too formal? Fix it first. Tap Edit prompt before you record — not after. Once something is recorded, changing the text just means a retake.
  4. Listen back before moving on. Use Replay to check it sounds right, Retake if it doesn't.
  5. Skip a sentence that doesn't make sense. Say why, don't force it.
  6. Review before anything leaves your phone. Flag a retake in the review queue — nothing uploads on its own.
  7. Sync only when you choose to. Tap Sync now. Nothing leaves the phone until you do.
  8. A person checks every batch. Your recordings become a pull request; someone reviews it before it trains anything.
TRACKING PROGRESS
  • The review queue shows your total recorded time against a 2-hour baseline — reaching it isn't a finish line, keep going if you have time.
  • Tap "Script progress" (on the recording screen or review queue) to see how much is recorded in each topic area. The one needing the most is always listed first.
  • The Script screen also lets you add your own sentences or fix wording across the whole list, any time — not just before you start.
CONSENT IS NOT ONE AND DONE
  • You can withdraw at any time. Say so, and your raw recordings are deleted.
  • This voice is for Smart-Q only — never resold or reused elsewhere without asking you again, separately.
  • Smart-Q tells its own users when they're hearing a synthetic voice, not you live.

Three things not to do

  • Don't record in a noisy room or with music nearby. Background noise gets learned as part of the voice.
  • Don't perform or polish your voice. Ordinary and natural is what makes it useful.
  • Don't skip the review queue. Check it before every sync — that's your last look before a person reviews it too.
